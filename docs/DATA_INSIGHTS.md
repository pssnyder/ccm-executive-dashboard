## Economic Cycle Analysis

### Key Takeaways on Oscillation

*   **Symmetrical Exchange:** Every time the economy transitions into a **Boom** (21 times), it transitions out of a **Boom** and back to **Normal** the exact same number of times (21 times).
*   **Recession Cycle:** The same symmetrical pattern is observed for **Recession**, with 18 transitions from **Normal** to **Recession** and 18 transitions from **Recession** back to **Normal**.
*   **No Direct Extreme Jumps:** In the data, there are no cycles showing direct transitions from 'Boom' to 'Recession' or 'Recession' to 'Boom'. The **Normal** phase acts as the required intermediate phase for the oscillation.

### Duration of Cycles (Days per Event)

While the number of cycles is symmetrical, the time spent in each phase (the duration in days) still varies:

*   **Normal:** The average cycle length is the longest at **17.58 days** (Min: 7 days, Max: 49 days).
*   **Recession:** The average cycle length is **14.78 days** (Min: 7 days, Max: 35 days).
*   **Boom:** The shortest average cycle length is **10.29 days** (Min: 7 days, Max: 35 days).

The data shows that the **Boom** phase, despite having the same number of exit cycles as entry cycles (21), is **shorter** on average than the **Recession** phase, making the positive extreme phase generally quicker than the negative one.
